# Choose your subjects for enrollment

## Overview
Search the subjects offered this term, pick a section for each, and build your proposed load in the **Enrollment summary**. Nothing is sent until you submit.

## Before you begin
- Enrollment is open for your year level.
- Know the subjects you need to take this term.

## Steps
1. Select **Enrollment** in the sidebar.
   → The **Choose subjects to enroll** page opens. The current term is shown at the top right.
2. To find a subject, choose what to search in (**All fields**, **Code**, **Subject name**, **Department**, or **Section**), then type in **Search subjects**.
3. Select a row to choose that section. Select it again to unselect it.
   → The selected rows are highlighted, and the count of "sections selected" goes up.
4. Select **Add selected subjects**.
   → The subjects appear in your **Enrollment summary**, and **Total selected units** updates.
5. To take a subject out, select the **×** next to it in the summary. To clear everything, select **Remove all**, then **Remove all** again to confirm.

When your summary is ready, [submit your enrollment](submit-your-enrollment.md).

### Reading the table
| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Schedule | Meeting days and times (M, T, W, TH, F, S) |
| Lec/Lab | Lecture units / laboratory units |
| Slots | Seats taken out of capacity, e.g. `28 / 40`. Your own pick counts once it's in your summary. |

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| A row says **Already added** | That subject is already in your summary. Remove it first if you want a different section. |
| A row says **Full** | No seats are left. Choose another section. |
| A row says **Reserved** or "Reserved until …" | That section is held for its own department until the date shown. Choose another section or wait. |
| "Adding these subjects would bring your load to … units, above the …-unit limit." | Remove subjects from your summary or select fewer. |
| "These schedules overlap" | The section you picked meets at the same time as one in your summary. Select **Review sections** and pick a different section. |
| "No subjects match your search." | Check the spelling, or switch **Search in** to **All fields**. |
| "The selected subjects are already in your enrollment summary." | Nothing new to add. |
