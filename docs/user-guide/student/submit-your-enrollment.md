# Submit your enrollment

## Overview
Send your chosen subjects to your Department Chair for review. They will approve your enrollment or return it with feedback.

## Before you begin
- Your subjects are in the **Enrollment summary** ([Choose your subjects for enrollment](choose-your-subjects.md)).
- Your load is within the unit limit shown under **Total selected units**.

## Steps
1. On the **Enrollment** page, review your **Enrollment summary** and total units.
2. Select **Submit**.
   → PIT Enroll checks prerequisites, corequisites, available slots, your unit limit, and schedule overlaps.
3. If everything passes, you'll see a confirmation that your enrollment was submitted.
4. Watch your **Dashboard** for the Department Chair's decision ([Check your enrollment status](check-your-enrollment-status.md)). Approved subjects appear in [Subjects Enrolled](view-your-enrolled-subjects.md).

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Submit** is unavailable | Your summary is empty. Add subjects first. |
| "Subject requirements not met" | A subject needs a prerequisite you haven't completed, or a corequisite you didn't add. Select **Review subjects**, then remove that subject or add the missing corequisite. |
| "No slots left" | The section filled up before you submitted. Remove it and choose another section. |
| "Your load is … units, above the …-unit limit." | Remove subjects until you're within the limit. |
| "These schedules overlap" | Two subjects in your summary meet at the same time. Select **Review sections** and choose a different section. |
