# View your grades

## Overview
Review your published midterm and final grades, subject status, and semester GPA for any semester.

## Before you begin
- You're logged in as a student.

## Steps
1. Select **Academic Records** in the sidebar.
2. Open the semester menu (top right) and choose a semester. Semesters are grouped by academic year.
   → A table shows **Subject code**, **Subject title**, **Midterm grade**, **Finals grade**, and **Status**, with your **semester GPA** above it.
3. To check for newly published grades, select **Reload records**.

[Screenshot: The Academic Records page]

### What you'll see
| You see | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **PENDING** | The grade hasn't been published yet. It is not a 0 or a failing grade. |
| A number, e.g. `1.75` | A published grade |
| **PASSED** / **FAILED** | The subject's result once both grades are published |
| **INC** | Incomplete |
| **DROPPED** | You withdrew from the subject |
| GPA shows **—** | No GPA yet, because no grades are published for that semester |

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "No academic records are available for this semester." | You have no records for that semester. Choose another one. |
| A grade still shows PENDING | Your Department Chair hasn't published it yet. Check back later. |
| A grade looks wrong | Contact your Department Chair, who keeps your grade records. |
