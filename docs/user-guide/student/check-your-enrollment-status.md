# Check your enrollment status

## Overview
See where your enrollment stands and read any feedback from your Department Chair. Check this after you submit your subjects.

## Before you begin
- You're logged in as a student.

## Steps
1. Select **Dashboard** in the sidebar.
2. Look at the two status cards:
   - **Student status**, for example *Regular*.
   - **Enrollment status**, for example *Returned* with "Changes requested by the Department Chair".
3. If your enrollment was returned, read the Department Chair's message in the feedback card. It shows when it was returned.
   → If there's no feedback, the card says "No enrollment feedback from your Department Chair yet."
4. Use the **Quick actions** cards to jump to Academic records, Student account, or Instructor evaluation.

[Screenshot: The Student Dashboard with status cards and feedback]

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Your enrollment was returned | Read the feedback, go to **Enrollment**, fix your subjects, and submit again ([Submit your enrollment](submit-your-enrollment.md)). |
| You don't understand the feedback | Contact your Department Chair. |
