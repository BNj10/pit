# Evaluate your instructor

## Overview
Rate your instructor's teaching for a subject during the evaluation period. Your evaluation is anonymous; faculty see only combined results after the evaluation period.

## Before you begin
- The evaluation period is open.
- Set aside a few minutes. Your answers are not saved until you submit.

## Steps
1. Select **Instructor Evaluation** in the sidebar.
   → A list shows each instructor with the subject and section.
2. Select **Evaluate** next to the instructor.
   → The **Student Evaluation of Teaching** form opens.
3. For every benchmark statement, choose one rating on the scale.
4. Optional: write **Recommendations and Suggestions** (up to 5,000 characters). Please be specific and respectful.
5. Select **Submit evaluation**.
   → You'll see "Evaluation submitted for [instructor]." and the list shows **Submitted** for that instructor.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Please rate all … statements before submitting." | Scroll up and rate each statement you skipped. |
| "Leave this evaluation?" | You tried to leave with unsaved answers. Select **Keep evaluating** to stay, or **Leave page** to discard them. |
| "This evaluation window is no longer open. Your answers have been kept here." | The evaluation period closed. Contact your Department Chair. |
| "You have already submitted this instructor's evaluation for this term." | Each instructor can be evaluated once per term. |
| "Unable to submit your evaluation. Your responses have been kept. Please try again." | Select **Submit evaluation** again. |
