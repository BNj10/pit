# View your enrolled subjects

## Overview
See the subjects your Department Chair approved for this term, with sections, classrooms, instructors, and units.

## Before you begin
- Your enrollment has been approved.

## Steps
1. Select **Subjects Enrolled** in the sidebar.
   → A table lists each approved subject: **Code / Subject title**, **Section**, **Classroom**, **Instructor**, **Lec/Lab** units, and **Total**.

[Screenshot: The Subjects Enrolled table]

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "You're not enrolled yet" | Your enrollment hasn't been approved. Select **Go to Enrollment** to choose and submit subjects, or check your [enrollment status](check-your-enrollment-status.md). |
| A subject you chose is missing | Only subjects approved by your Department Chair are shown. Contact your Department Chair. |
