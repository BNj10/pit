# Find a student's records

## Overview
Look up a student in your department and open their academic records.

## Before you begin
- You're logged in as a Department Chair.

## Steps
1. Select **Student Records** in the sidebar.
   → The **Students** list shows name, university email, student ID, year standing, and batch.
2. Narrow the list:
   - Type in **Search by name, email, or student number**.
   - Use the **year level** and **batch** filters.
3. Select **Records** on the student's row.
   → Their **Academic Records** page opens with semester grades and GPA.
4. To view another semester, choose it from **Academic semester**.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "No students found." | Clear the search and set the filters back to **All year levels** and **All batches**. |
| "No grades published yet" | That semester has no published grades. You can add them if it's the current school year ([Enter and publish grades](enter-and-publish-grades.md)). |
