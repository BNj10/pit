# Archive a curriculum

## Overview
Retire a draft curriculum while keeping it and its subjects for reference. You can restore it later.

## Before you begin
- The curriculum is a **Draft**. If it's Active, [deactivate it](change-curriculum-status.md) first.

## Steps
1. Select **Curricula and Subjects** in the sidebar.
2. Select **Archive** on the curriculum's row.
   → The **Archive this curriculum?** dialog shows how many subjects will be preserved.
3. Select **Archive curriculum**.
   → You'll see "[name] was archived." It now appears under the **Archived** tab and is read-only.

To bring it back, select **Restore to draft** on its row.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| There's no **Archive** button | Only Draft curricula can be archived. Deactivate an Active curriculum first. |
