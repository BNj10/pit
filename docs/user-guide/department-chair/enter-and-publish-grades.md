# Enter and publish grades

## Overview
Record a student's midterm and final grades, save them as a draft, and publish them when they're final. Students only see published grades.

## Before you begin
- The semester is in the **current school year**. Completed school years are view-only.

## Steps
1. [Find the student's records](find-a-student-record.md) and choose the current semester.
2. Select **Edit Grades** (or **Edit Draft** if a draft already exists).
   → You'll see "Editing grades. Changes stay in draft until published."
3. Select a midterm or finals grade cell.
4. In the dialog, choose a grade:
   - A number from **1.00** to **3.00** in steps of 0.25, or **5.00**
   - Under **Other outcomes**: **Incomplete** (INC) or **Dropped** (DROP)
5. Select **Add grade** or **Save grade**.
   → The subject's status is recalculated (PASSED, FAILED, INC, or DROPPED).
6. Repeat for other subjects, then select **Save Draft**.
   → The badge shows **Draft** ("Not visible to students").
7. When the grades are final, select **Publish Grades**.
   → The badge shows **Published** ("Visible to the student").

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| There's no **Edit Grades** button | The semester belongs to a completed school year and is view-only. |
| **Publish Grades** is unavailable | Add at least one grade first. |
| "No grade changes. The published grades are unchanged." | Nothing was changed since the last publish. |
| **Add grade** is unavailable | Choose a grade first. |
| You're not sure about a grade value | Check your institution's grading policy before publishing. |
