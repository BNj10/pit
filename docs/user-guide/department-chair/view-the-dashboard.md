# View the Department Chair Dashboard

## Overview
See your department at a glance: student records, pending enrollments, active sections, and active curricula, with shortcuts to each area.

## Before you begin
- You're logged in as a Department Chair.

## Steps
1. Select **Dashboard** in the sidebar.
   → The **Department overview** shows:
   - **Student records** within your department
   - **Pending enrollments** awaiting your review
   - **Active sections** in the current academic term
   - **Active curricula** available for management
2. Under **Quick access**, select a card to go straight to that area.

Pending enrollments are the most time-sensitive. See [Approve or return a student's enrollment](review-an-enrollment.md).
