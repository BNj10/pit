# Add subjects to offer in a semester

## Overview
Choose which approved curriculum subjects your department offers in a semester for a year level. These are the subjects you'll then [add sections](add-or-edit-a-section.md) to.

## Before you begin
- The Dean has created the academic year's terms.
- The subjects are in an **Active** curriculum.

## Steps
1. Select **Sections** in the sidebar.
   → The **Manage Sections** page opens.
2. Choose the **Academic year** and the **Curriculum year level**.
3. Select **Add semester** and choose the semester or summer term.
   → You'll see "[term] added to [year level]."
4. In that semester, select **Add subjects**.
5. Choose the **Curriculum** if needed (the latest active one is shown first), then tick the subjects to offer.
6. Select **Add [n] subjects**.
   → You'll see "[n] subjects added to [term]."

New subjects start reserved for your department's students. See [Set when other departments can enroll](set-cross-department-access.md).

### Removing a subject or semester
- **Remove subject**: removes the subject and all its section entries from that term.
- **Remove semester**: removes the term and everything in it for that year level.
Both ask you to confirm first.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "No academic terms have been created for [year] yet. A Dean creates them." | Ask the Dean to create the academic year. |
| "No semesters yet" | Select **Add semester** first. |
| A subject is missing from the list | It may be in a Draft curriculum. Activate the curriculum, or choose another curriculum in the dialog. |
