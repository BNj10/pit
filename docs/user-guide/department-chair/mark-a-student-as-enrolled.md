# Mark a student as enrolled

## Overview
Finish a student's enrollment once their Certificate of Enrollment has every required signature. After this, their subjects are final for the term.

## Before you begin
- You approved the student's enrollment ([Approve or return a student's enrollment](review-an-enrollment.md)).
- The student's Certificate of Enrollment has all required signatures.

## Steps
1. Select **Enrollment Management** and find the student (status **Approved**).
2. Select **Preview** on their row.
3. Select **Mark as enrolled**.
   → The **Mark as enrolled?** dialog asks you to confirm the signatures are complete.
4. Confirm.
   → You'll see "[student] marked as enrolled." The status becomes **Enrolled**.

[Screenshot: The Mark as enrolled confirmation]

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| There's no **Mark as enrolled** button | The enrollment isn't approved yet. Approve it first. |
| You marked the wrong student | This can't be undone from this page. Contact the Administrator. |
