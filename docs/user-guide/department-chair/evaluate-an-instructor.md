# Evaluate an instructor in your department

## Overview
Complete the Department Chair's Evaluation of Faculty for each instructor in your department. Rate their overall handling of assigned subjects, not each subject separately.

## Before you begin
- The Dean has opened the evaluation period.
- Gather your evidence. Each statement lists suggested means for verification.

## Steps
1. Select **Instructor Evaluation** in the sidebar.
   → Your department's instructors are listed.
2. Find the instructor. Use **Search instructors by name** or the evaluation status filter.
3. Select **Evaluate**.
   → The **Department Chair's Evaluation of Faculty** form opens.
4. For each benchmark statement, choose a rating: **Always**, **Often**, **Sometimes**, **Seldom**, or **Never**.
5. Optional: add **Recommendations and Suggestions**.
6. Submit the evaluation.
   → You'll see "Evaluation submitted for [instructor]." The row now offers **View results**.

Submitted evaluations are locked. To review one, see [View your evaluation of an instructor](view-your-instructor-evaluation.md).

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Instructor evaluation isn't available right now" | The evaluation period isn't open. Wait for the Dean to open it. |
| "No instructors to evaluate" | No instructors are assigned to your department this term. |
| "Select a rating for every statement." | Rate every statement before submitting. |
| "Leave this evaluation?" | You tried to leave with unsaved ratings. Select **Keep evaluating** to stay. |
| "You have already submitted this instructor's evaluation for this term." | Each instructor is evaluated once per term. |
