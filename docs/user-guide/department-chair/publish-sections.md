# Save and publish sections

## Overview
Save your section plan as a draft while you work, then publish it so students in that year level can see the subjects and sections when they enroll.

## Before you begin
- You've added subjects and sections for the year level.

## Steps
1. Select **Sections** and choose the academic year and year level.
2. Check the bar at the bottom of the page:
   - *Unsaved changes*: you have changes that aren't saved yet.
   - *Not visible to students until published*.
3. Select **Save draft** to keep your work without showing it to students.
   → You'll see "[year level] draft saved."
4. When it's ready, select **Publish [year level]**.
   → A confirmation dialog shows how many subjects and section entries students will see.
5. Confirm.
   → You'll see "[year level] sections published for [academic year]."

After publishing, students keep seeing the last published version until you publish new changes.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Add at least one subject to [year level] before publishing." | [Add subjects to offer](add-subjects-to-offer.md) first. |
| "Schedule the weekly hours each subject's units require (1 unit = 1 hr): …" | Publishing is blocked until the sections listed have enough weekly hours for their units. [Edit those sections](add-or-edit-a-section.md). |
| "[n] section entries have no faculty member assigned." | A warning: you can still publish, but consider assigning faculty first. |
| **Save draft** is unavailable | There are no unsaved changes. |
