# View your evaluation of an instructor

## Overview
Review the ratings you submitted for an instructor this term.

## Before you begin
- You've submitted an evaluation for the instructor.

## Steps
1. Select **Instructor Evaluation** in the sidebar.
2. Select **View results** on the instructor's row.
   → Your submitted **Department Chair's Evaluation of Faculty** is shown, read-only.
3. Select **Back to Instructor Evaluation** to return to the list.

[Screenshot: A submitted Department Chair evaluation]

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "No submitted evaluation" | You haven't evaluated this instructor this term. [Evaluate them](evaluate-an-instructor.md). |
