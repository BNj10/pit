# Set when other departments can enroll

## Overview
Decide whether students from other departments can take the remaining seats in a subject you offer, and from when.

## Before you begin
- The subject is offered in a semester on the **Sections** page.

## Steps
1. Select **Sections** and expand the semester and the subject.
2. Select the subject's access setting. It shows the current setting, such as *Open to all departments* or *Own department until [date]*.
   → The **Other departments** dialog opens.
3. Under **When other departments can enroll**, choose one:
   - **Keep reserved for [your department]**: only your students can enroll until you change this.
   - **Open now**: other departments can take remaining seats right away.
   - **Open on a date**: seats open automatically on the date and time you set in **Opens on** (Philippine time).
4. Select **Save access**.
   → You'll see "[code] access for other departments updated."

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Choose an opening date and time." / **Save access** is unavailable | Fill in both the date and the time for **Open on a date**. |
