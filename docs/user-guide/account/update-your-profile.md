# Update your profile details

## Overview
Keep your addresses and contact details up to date. Your name, ID, and academic or employment details are maintained by the Administrator and can't be changed here.

## Before you begin
- You're logged in as a student, Department Chair, or Dean.

## Steps
1. Open your account page: **Student Account**, **Department Chair Account**, or **Account** at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Select **Edit profile**.
3. Update any of these:
   - **Current address**
   - **Permanent address**
   - **Email address**
   - **Mobile number**
4. Select **Save changes**.
   → You'll see a message that your profile was updated, and the page shows your new details.

To stop without saving, select **Cancel**.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Your name or ID can't be edited | These are read-only. Contact the Administrator if they're wrong. |
| Mobile number isn't accepted (Department Chair and Dean) | Enter 7 to 25 characters using digits, spaces, `+`, parentheses, or hyphens. |
| **Change password** is unavailable | Save or cancel your profile changes first. To change your password, use [Reset your password](reset-your-password.md). |
