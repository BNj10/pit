# Set up your account

## Overview
Finish your new account the first time you use PIT Enroll. You'll choose a password, and students also register their student ID. You can't use the portal until this is done.

## Before you begin
- The invitation email from PIT Enroll. The link expires after a while (1 hour by default).
- Students: your student ID, in the format `00-0000-000`.

## Steps
1. Open the invitation email and select the link in it.
   → The **Set up your account** page opens and shows the email you're signed in as.
2. Students only: enter your **student ID**.
3. Enter a **new password** (at least 6 characters).
4. Enter the same password again in **Confirm password**.
5. Select **Finish setup**.
   → You'll be taken to your dashboard.

From now on, students can log in with their email or their student ID, using the same password. Staff log in with their email.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "This link has expired or was already used…" on the login page | Ask the registrar's office to send a new invitation. |
| "Enter your student ID in the format 00-0000-000." | Include the dashes, e.g. `22-1234-567`. |
| "That student ID is already registered. Check it, or contact the registrar's office." | Check for typing mistakes. If the ID is right, contact the registrar's office. |
| "Both passwords must match." | Retype the password the same way in both fields. |
| Password is too short | Use at least 6 characters. |
| "Unable to save right now. Please try again." | Select **Finish setup** again. Anything already saved is kept. |
| This isn't your email | Select **Log out** on the page, then open the right invitation link. |
