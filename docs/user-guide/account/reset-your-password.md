# Reset your password

## Overview
Choose a new password when you've forgotten yours. PIT Enroll emails you a link to set it.

## Before you begin
- The email address of your PIT Enroll account, and access to that inbox.

## Steps
1. On the login page, select **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your **email address** and select **Send reset instructions**.
   → You'll see a confirmation that the request was sent.
3. Open the email from PIT Enroll and select the reset link.
   → The **Set new password** page opens.
4. Enter your **new password**, then enter it again to confirm.
5. Select **Reset password**.
   → You'll see that your password was updated, and you're logged out.
6. Select **Back to login** and log in with your new password.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| No email arrives | Check your spam folder, and make sure you typed the email of your PIT Enroll account. For privacy, the confirmation page looks the same whether or not the email has an account. |
| "Please enter your email address." or "Please enter a valid email address (e.g. name@example.com)." | Enter your full email address and check it for typing mistakes. |
| "This link has expired or was already used…" | Request a new reset link and use the newest email. |
| "Choose a password different from your current one." | Pick a password you haven't been using. |
| "Both passwords must match." | Retype the same password in both fields. |
| Password is too short | Use at least 6 characters. |
