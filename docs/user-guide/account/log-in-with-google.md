# Log in with Google

## Overview
Sign in using your Google account instead of typing a password. It works only if your Google email is the same email as your PIT Enroll account.

## Before you begin
- A PIT Enroll account that uses the same email address as your Google account.
- Google sign-in turned on for your school's PIT Enroll site.

## Steps
1. Open PIT Enroll.
2. Select **Continue with Google**.
   → The button shows "Redirecting to Google…" and Google's sign-in page opens.
3. Choose your Google account (or sign in to Google), and allow access if asked.
4. Wait to be brought back to PIT Enroll.
   → You'll be taken to your dashboard. If your account setup isn't finished, you'll see **Set up your account** first.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "This account has no portal access yet. Contact the registrar's office." | The Google email you picked has no PIT Enroll account. Choose the Google account that uses your school email, or log in with your email and password. |
| "Unable to log in with Google. Please try again." | Try again. If it keeps happening, log in with your email and password. |
| You went back from Google's page | That's fine. The login page is ready to use again. |
