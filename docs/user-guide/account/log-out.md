# Log out

## Overview
End your session so nobody else can use your account on this device. Always log out on shared or public computers.

## Before you begin
- You're logged in.

## Steps
1. Find **Log out** (students) or **Logout** (Department Chairs and Deans) at the bottom of the sidebar. On a phone, open the menu first.
2. Select it.
   → You'll be taken back to the login page.

[Screenshot: The Log out button in the sidebar]

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Unable to sign out. Please try again." | Select **Log out** again. If it keeps failing, close the browser. |
| You're still filling in an instructor evaluation | Logging out discards unsubmitted answers. Submit the evaluation first. |
