# Log in with your email or student ID

## Overview
Sign in to PIT Enroll with your password. Students can use either their email address or their student ID; Department Chairs and Deans use their email address.

## Before you begin
- An account created for you by the Administrator.
- Your account setup finished (see [Set up your account](set-up-your-account.md)).
- Students who want to log in with their ID: the student ID you entered during setup.

## Steps
1. Open PIT Enroll.
   → The login page opens.
2. In **Email or ID number**, enter your email address, or your student ID in the format `00-0000-000`.
3. Enter your **password**. Select the eye icon if you want to check what you typed.
4. Select **Log in**.
   → You'll be taken to your dashboard: the Student, Department Chair, or Dean Dashboard.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Invalid login credentials." | Check the email or student ID and the password, and check Caps Lock. If you've forgotten your password, use **Forgot password?** ([Reset your password](reset-your-password.md)). |
| "Enter your email or ID number." | Enter a full email address, or a student ID with dashes, like `22-1234-567`. |
| "Too many login attempts. Wait a few minutes, then try again." | Wait a few minutes before trying again. |
| "This account has no portal access yet. Contact the registrar's office." | Your sign-in exists but has no portal role yet. Contact the registrar's office. |
| "Unable to log in right now. Please try again." | Something went wrong on our side. Try again in a moment. |
| You're sent to **Set up your account** instead of your dashboard | Your account setup isn't finished. Complete it first. |
| Your student ID isn't accepted | You can only log in with an ID after you've set it during account setup. Use your email instead. |
