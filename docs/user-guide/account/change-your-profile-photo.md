# Change your profile photo

## Overview
Add or replace the photo shown on your account page.

## Before you begin
- A PNG, JPG, or WebP image of 2 MB or smaller. A square image looks best, because the photo is shown in a circle.

## Steps
1. Open your account page from the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Select your profile picture (**Edit profile photo**).
3. Select **Choose image** and pick a file, or drag the file onto the box.
   → A preview of your photo appears.
4. Select **Save photo**.
   → Your new photo appears on your account page.

To go back to no photo, select **Remove photo**, then **Save photo**. To keep your current photo, select **Cancel**.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Choose a PNG, JPG or WebP image." | Convert or export the picture to one of those formats. |
| "Choose an image that is 2 MB or smaller." | Use a smaller or more compressed image. |
| "This image could not be read." or "This image could not be displayed." | The file may be damaged. Choose another image. |
