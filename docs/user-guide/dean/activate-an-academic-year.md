# Activate an academic year

## Overview
Make a draft academic year the active one, so its terms can start and its enrollment windows can be set. Only one academic year can be active at a time.

## Before you begin
- The year is a draft ([Create or edit an academic year](create-an-academic-year.md)).
- No other year is active. If one is, [complete it](complete-an-academic-year.md) first.

## Steps
1. Select **Enrollment Management**, then **Academic years & terms**.
2. Under **All academic years**, select **Activate year** on the year's row.
   → The dialog explains that the year becomes active.
3. Select **Activate year**.
   → You'll see "Academic year [year] activated." It appears under **Active academic year**.

Next: [Start the next term](start-the-next-term.md) when classes begin.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Mark [year] completed before activating [year]. Only one academic year can be active." | Complete the current active year first. |
| "Only a draft academic year can be activated." | That year is already active or completed. |
