# View the Dean Dashboard

## Overview
Get a quick overview of your college: active academic units, departments, faculty members, and enrolled students, with shortcuts to common tasks.

## Before you begin
- You're logged in as a Dean.

## Steps
1. Select **Dashboard** in the sidebar.
   → The **School overview** shows active academic units, departments across all colleges, active faculty members, and enrolled students for the academic year.
2. Under **Quick actions**, select:
   - **Manage colleges & departments** to review the academic structure ([Add a college](add-a-college.md))
   - **Faculty evaluations** to monitor evaluation progress ([Set the faculty evaluation window](set-the-evaluation-window.md))
