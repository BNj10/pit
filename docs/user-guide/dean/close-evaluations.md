# Close faculty evaluations

## Overview
End the semester's evaluation period once every evaluation is in. The term's results then move to **Previous semester results**.

## Before you begin
- Every faculty member has all their student, Department Chair, and College Dean evaluations. Check the completion figures on the **Semester evaluation window** card.

## Steps
1. Select **Faculty Evaluation** in the sidebar.
2. On the **Semester evaluation window** card, select **Close evaluations**.
   → The **Close evaluations now?** dialog opens.
3. Select **Close evaluations**. To cancel, select **Keep window open**.
   → You'll see "Evaluations closed." The term appears in **Previous semester results**.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Evaluations can be closed once every faculty member has all student, Department Chair, and College Dean evaluations." | Check the completion figures and follow up on missing evaluations. Complete your own with [Evaluate a faculty member](evaluate-a-faculty-member.md). |
| You need to reopen a closed term | [Edit the window](set-the-evaluation-window.md) for the same term and save it again. |
