# Complete an academic year

## Overview
Close the active academic year at the end of Summer. All its terms become completed, the year moves to history, and you can activate the next one.

## Before you begin
- The year is active and **Summer** is its current term.

## Steps
1. Select **Enrollment Management**, then **Academic years & terms**.
2. Under **Active academic year**, select **Mark completed**.
   → The **Mark [year] completed?** dialog opens.
3. Select **Mark completed**.
   → You'll see "Academic year [year] completed."

Next: [Activate the next academic year](activate-an-academic-year.md).

[Screenshot: The Mark completed confirmation]

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Start Summer for [year] first. An academic year can be marked completed only while Summer is the current term." | [Start the Summer term](start-the-next-term.md), then complete the year. |
| "Only the active academic year can be completed." | Choose the year that is currently active. |
