# Pause or resume enrollment

## Overview
Stop enrollment for all year levels at once without changing the scheduled windows, then resume it when ready.

## Before you begin
- An enrollment schedule has been published ([Set the enrollment schedule](set-the-enrollment-schedule.md)).

## Steps
1. Select **Enrollment Management**, then **Enrollment schedule**.
2. Under **Need to stop enrollment?**, select **Pause enrollment**.
   → The dialog explains that students can't enroll until you resume.
3. Select **Pause enrollment**.
   → You'll see "Enrollment paused." The card shows **Enrollment is paused**.
4. To restart, select **Resume enrollment**, then confirm.
   → You'll see "Enrollment resumed." Windows that are still within their scheduled time reopen.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| A group didn't reopen after resuming | Its window has already closed. [Change its dates](set-the-enrollment-schedule.md) and publish again. |
