# View a department's faculty and students

## Overview
See who teaches in a department and which students belong to it. These directories are view-only.

## Before you begin
- You're logged in as a Dean.

## Steps
1. Select **Colleges & Departments**, then **Manage departments** on the college's card.
2. On the department's row:
   - Select **Faculty** to see its instructors with their department ranking. Use the search box to find someone.
   - Select **Students** to see its students.
3. In the student list, select a student to open their **Student account** details (university email, year standing, and more).

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "No faculty match your search." | Check the spelling or clear the search. |
| You're looking for a student's grades | Deans don't have access to student grades. The student's Department Chair keeps their records. |
