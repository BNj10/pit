# Department Chairs invite students to their own department

ADR 0001 had the Administrator create every account. We moved student accounts to the Department Chair: a chair adds a student to their own department, and that same step sends the student an invitation to set up their account, so no student exists without one. The chair can resend an invitation until the student finishes account setup. Chairs already keep their students' records and grades, so they know who belongs in the department; going through the Administrator for every new student was a bottleneck. The Administrator still creates Department Chair and Dean accounts and registers faculty member profiles. Revisit this decision before letting chairs add students to another department, invite staff, or add students without sending an invitation.
